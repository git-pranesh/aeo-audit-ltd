THE PROBLEM:
The `COMPETITOR_BENCHMARKS` object is a single static set of SaaS-level numbers
used for ALL industries. A bakery, a dental clinic, a restaurant, a gold mining 
company are all scored against SaaS benchmarks. This causes thin-content 
non-tech sites to score F unfairly.

THE FIX:
Replace the single `COMPETITOR_BENCHMARKS` object with an 
industry-aware benchmark lookup. Here is the exact implementation:
