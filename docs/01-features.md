# Citation Rocket — Feature Overview

_Written in plain, everyday language — the way it would appear on the marketing site._

---

## The big idea

Your customers have stopped Googling. They're asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude *"what's the best tool for X?"* — and those AI assistants are quietly recommending someone. If it's not you, you're losing deals you never even knew existed.

**Citation Rocket shows you exactly why AI isn't recommending your business — and hands you the plan to change that.**

Paste your website. In a couple of minutes you get a full AI Readiness Report: your score, who's beating you, and the precise steps to start showing up in AI answers.

---

## What you get

### 🎯 Your AI Readiness Score
A single, easy-to-read score from **0–100** (with a letter grade A–F) that tells you how "AI-friendly" your website is. No jargon — just a clear number you can track over time.

### 🤖 Scores for each AI platform
Not every AI thinks alike. You get **separate scores for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude**, so you know where you're strong and where you're invisible.

### 🔍 The AI Citation Snapshot (the "moment of truth")
This is the feature that makes people gasp. We literally **ask AI the questions your customers ask** — things like *"best [your category] for [your use case]"* — and show you:
- **Your Mention Rate** — how often AI actually names you.
- **The exact prompts** we tested, and whether you showed up in each one.
- **Which competitors got recommended instead of you.**

You see, in black and white, who AI is sending your customers to.

### 🧭 Content Signal Breakdown
We check your site for the specific "signals" AI looks for when deciding who to trust — tutorials, case studies, FAQs, data points, frameworks, structured data (schema), how-to guides, and authority links. You see which ones you have and which you're missing.

### 📊 Industry Benchmarks
Your scores are compared against **realistic benchmarks for your specific industry** — so a law firm isn't unfairly judged against a software company. You see how you stack up against the norm.

### 🛠️ The Fix-It Playbook
This is your action plan. Instead of vague advice, you get **specific, prioritized steps** — including ready-to-use content templates and code snippets — to close the gaps that are holding you back. Higher plans get a deeper, prioritized version (Enterprise's is also unbranded).

### ✅ Quick Wins Checklist
The handful of **highest-impact things you can do right now**, pulled out of the playbook into a simple checkbox list. Do these first.

### 💡 AI-Ready Topic Ideas
Fresh content ideas grouped into four buckets — **Authoritative, Thought Leadership, Competitive Advantage, and Emerging Opportunity** — designed specifically to earn AI citations. The number of ideas scales with your plan.

### 🔁 Re-Audits & Progress Tracking
Made improvements? Re-run the audit and watch your score climb. Citation Rocket **remembers your progress**, keeps your completed checklist items ticked, and archives things you've already fixed.

### 📡 Ongoing Citation Monitoring
Citation Rocket keeps watching. On a schedule that matches your plan (monthly, weekly, or daily), it **automatically re-checks whether AI is mentioning you** and can **email you an alert** when your mention rate moves.

### 📄 Professional PDF Reports
Export a polished report to share with your team or clients. Higher plans get an **unbranded** report — Citation Rocket's branding is removed. (Note: it does not add your own logo; it's unbranded, not custom-branded.)

### 📥 Downloadable AI-Ready Files (AEO Pack)
Generate a ready-to-install ZIP of the files AI crawlers look for: `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `AGENTS.md`, a `robots.txt` patch, five schema files (Organization, FAQ, How-To, Article, Breadcrumbs), a clean markdown copy of your homepage, and install instructions. **Pro plan and above only** (Pro, Enterprise, Lifetime) — the server blocks Free and Starter with an "Upgrade required" message. (Separate from this ZIP: the audit report itself shows copy-paste schema snippets — Free sees 1 snippet, all paid plans see all of them.)

### 📊 CSV & Score-Trend Export
Export your full audit data (scores, findings, competitors, topics, citation data) as a CSV — **available on every plan, including Free** (the download button has no plan gate). Separately, the **score trend over time** export is Pro and above.

### 👀 Competitor Watchlist
Keep a list of named competitors and track them over time. The number you can track scales with your plan (Starter 3; Pro/Enterprise/Lifetime unlimited).

### 🌐 Multiple Websites
Run and manage audits across more than one domain — 1 site on Free, 5 on Starter, unlimited on Pro and above.

### 🔌 Developer API
Higher-tier accounts get REST API access with secure keys and a monthly call quota, so you can pull audit data into your own tools.

### 🧩 Chrome Extension
A companion browser extension runs a quick, live AI-readiness check on whatever page you're viewing — scoring things like answer clarity, schema, freshness, and whether the site blocks AI crawlers, right from your browser.

## Why it's different
Most SEO tools optimize for Google's blue links. Citation Rocket optimizes for the **new front door** — the AI assistant that answers before anyone ever sees a search result. It doesn't just score you; it **shows you the actual AI answers** and gives you the plan to win them.
